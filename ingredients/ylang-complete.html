Ylang Ylang Complete

Heart · Base

The unfractionated, full distillation of Cananga odorata, capturing the whole living flower in one oil - bright, jasmine-like floral top notes giving way to a creamy, banana-soft, balsamic depth. Because it is collected across the entire distillation rather than a single fraction, it is the most balanced and naturalistic representation of ylang ylang. Rich and complex, it works from heart to base and brings a rounded tropical opulence to florals and orientals.
